# 03 Offers

Canonical offer architecture for Andy Good as a **Senior Content & AI Strategist**.

## 1. Content, Messaging & Conversion Strategy

### Best-fit buyer

Founder, CMO, Head of Marketing or Head of Content at a SaaS, technology, AI or growth-focused business with a commercially important content, messaging or conversion problem.

### Typical trigger/problem

A launch, repositioning, website rebuild, campaign, weak conversion, unclear messaging, inconsistent content direction or a broader need to improve how content supports growth.

### Core commercial outcome

Clearer positioning and messaging, stronger conversion thinking, better content decisions and a practical strategic direction that improves commercial performance.

### What is included

- Senior diagnosis
- Strategic direction
- Messaging architecture
- Conversion strategy
- Content strategy
- Prioritisation around the defined commercial problem

### What may be included depending on scope

- Website or landing-page strategy
- Campaign strategy
- SEO or AI-search considerations
- Content planning
- Stakeholder workshops
- Review of existing assets
- Implementation guidance

### What is explicitly excluded

- Routine content production
- Commodity blog writing
- Ongoing editorial management
- Low-value standalone deliverables
- Generic AI automation
- Prompt engineering
- Broader operational AI work

### Hands-on execution boundary

Hands-on copy/content execution can belong naturally within the engagement when it is strategically important to solving the defined commercial problem.

### Natural expansion path

- **Offer 2: Fractional Content & AI Strategy** when the client needs ongoing senior direction, ownership and decision support.
- **Offer 3: AI-Enabled Content Systems** when the work reveals problems in workflow, knowledge, production, review or AI-assisted content operations.

### Proof/case studies needed

- Clear commercial problem
- Andy's strategic role, not just final deliverables
- Before/after messaging or conversion thinking
- Measurable outcomes where defensible
- Credible SaaS, technology, agency or growth-company context
- Evidence that strategy led to stronger execution or performance

## 2. Fractional Content & AI Strategy

### Best-fit buyer

Founder, CMO, Head of Marketing or Head of Content at a SaaS, technology, AI or growth-focused business that needs senior content leadership without a full-time senior hire.

### Typical trigger/problem

The business has ongoing content, messaging, campaign and AI-related decisions to make, but lacks consistent senior ownership. Activity is happening, but priorities, quality, commercial focus and ways of working are fragmented.

### Core commercial outcome

Stronger ongoing strategic direction, better decision-making, higher-quality execution across the team and more effective adoption of AI within content and marketing.

### What is included

- Ongoing content and messaging strategy
- Strategic prioritisation and planning
- Senior review and quality control
- Campaign and editorial direction
- AI-search and content implications
- Identification, prioritisation and oversight of improvements to content operations and AI-enabled workflows
- Guidance to internal teams, founders or agency partners

### What may be included depending on scope

- Stakeholder workshops
- Team enablement
- Content governance
- Strategic input into major launches
- Senior review of key assets
- Agency oversight
- Selective involvement in high-value execution

### What is explicitly excluded

- High-volume content production
- Routine copywriting retainers
- Acting as a junior execution resource
- Day-to-day social or editorial management
- Company-wide AI transformation
- General automation
- Unrelated marketing functions

### Hands-on execution boundary

Execution should be selective and exceptional, not an ongoing production commitment. Significant standalone copy/content execution should be scoped under Offer 1.

### Natural expansion path

- **Offer 1: Content, Messaging & Conversion Strategy** when a defined strategic problem needs deeper project-based intervention.
- **Offer 3: AI-Enabled Content Systems** when the work becomes a substantial audit, redesign or implementation of the content operating system.

### Proof/case studies needed

- Ongoing strategic responsibility
- Influence across multiple initiatives rather than one deliverable
- Work with founders, marketers, agencies or cross-functional teams
- Improvements in decision quality, consistency or performance
- Examples of raising standards over time
- Testimonials referencing judgement, ownership and senior contribution

### Offer 2 vs Offer 3 systems boundary

Offer 2 may identify, prioritise and oversee improvements to content operations and AI-enabled workflows.

If the work becomes a substantial audit, redesign or implementation of the content operating system, it belongs under Offer 3.

## 3. AI-Enabled Content Systems

### Best-fit buyer

Founder, CMO, Head of Marketing or Head of Content at a SaaS, technology, AI or growth-focused business with enough content activity for process, consistency and AI adoption to matter commercially.

### Typical trigger/problem

The team is producing content, often already using AI, but the underlying system is fragmented. Research, briefing, source knowledge, production, review, SEO/GEO, repurposing and governance do not work together cleanly.

### Core commercial outcome

A more effective content operating system that improves speed, consistency, quality and reuse while making AI genuinely useful rather than adding more tools.

### What is included

- Audit of the current content operation
- Identification of bottlenecks, duplication and quality risks
- Redesign of priority workflows
- Content knowledge and source architecture
- AI-assisted research, briefing, creation and review processes
- Editorial quality control and human-review points
- Practical implementation guidance and documentation

### What may be included depending on scope

- AI-search workflows
- Content libraries
- Governance standards
- Team enablement
- Reusable assistants
- Prompt architecture
- Repurposing systems
- Integration with existing marketing tools

### What is explicitly excluded

- Company-wide AI transformation
- General business automation
- Finance or operations workflows
- Custom software as the primary deliverable
- Generic no-code automation
- Low-cost GPT builds
- Tool implementation without a meaningful content or marketing problem

### Hands-on execution boundary

Hands-on work should be limited to prototyping, testing and validating the system with real content. It should not become content production.

### Natural expansion path

- **Offer 1: Content, Messaging & Conversion Strategy** when systems work exposes a deeper messaging, conversion or content-strategy problem.
- **Offer 2: Fractional Content & AI Strategy** when the client needs ongoing senior oversight, optimisation and strategic leadership after the system is in place.

### Proof/case studies needed

- The workflow or content-operation problem before intervention
- The redesigned system and Andy's strategic role
- How AI and human judgement work together
- Evidence of improved speed, consistency, throughput, reuse or quality
- Real workflow maps, documentation or system examples
- Measurable operational or commercial improvement where defensible

## Andy vs allmi boundary

Andy Good owns work where the primary problem is content, marketing, messaging, conversion, AI search, content operations, marketing knowledge systems, AI-enabled content and marketing workflows, or growth-related strategy and systems.

allmi owns broader practical AI implementation across the wider business, including operational automation, process redesign, company-wide AI implementation, internal business systems, custom applications and wider business efficiency.

**Boundary rule:** if the primary problem is content, marketing, messaging or growth, it belongs under Andy Good. If the primary problem is broader business operations, it belongs under allmi.
