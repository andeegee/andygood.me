# Andy Good | Positioning & Growth

## Project objective

Reposition Andy Good from being perceived primarily as a copywriter/content producer into a higher-value **Senior Content & AI Strategist**, while preserving and increasing the content, copywriting and strategy opportunities that already generate paid work.

At the same time, build **allmi** as a separate practical AI consultancy focused on broader business systems, workflow automation, custom solutions and operational efficiency.

The goal is not to abandon copywriting.

The goal is to move Andy higher up the value chain from producing individual deliverables to advising on and designing the strategy, systems and processes behind them.

## Core commercial strategy

Use Andy Good's existing reputation, experience and marketability as the primary near-term revenue engine.

Use allmi as the second growth curve.

The intended progression is:

**Content gets Andy in.
Strategy increases the value of the engagement.
AI systems expand the scope.
allmi develops a separate source of higher-value AI implementation work.**

Do not sacrifice proven revenue in an attempt to force an immediate transition into generic AI consulting.

Where Andy focuses primarily on marketing, content, communication and growth systems, allmi can work across the wider business.

## Positioning principle

Andy is not:

**An AI person who learned marketing.**

The stronger positioning is:

**An experienced marketing, content and conversion strategist who now understands how to redesign that work for the AI era.**

AI should enhance Andy's existing credibility rather than replace it.
