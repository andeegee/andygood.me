# 04 Visual Identity

## 1. Brand visual principle

Andy Good should feel:

- clean
- modern
- premium
- confident
- human
- senior
- content-led
- commercially sophisticated
- digitally sharp

The design should rely on typography, spacing, hierarchy and contrast rather than decorative motifs.

**Core principle:**  
**The copy is the personality. The design gives it authority.**

---

## 2. Colour palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Primary text / dark surface | Carbon | `#121820` |
| Secondary text | Slate | `#606A75` |
| Supporting surface | Fog | `#F3F5F7` |
| Supporting line / border | Steel | `#D9DEE3` |
| Signature accent | Signal Yellow | `#FFD400` |

Andy should be predominantly white/light, with Carbon providing authority and Signal Yellow providing the distinctive pop.

Use Signal Yellow sparingly for:

- emphasis
- action
- recognition
- short rules or edges
- small filled elements
- selected proof or result emphasis

Do not:

- use large yellow background fields by default
- use yellow body text
- introduce additional signature colours

Use Carbon text on Signal Yellow when text appears inside a yellow element. Do not use Signal Yellow for normal-sized text on White.

### Shared design DNA

- strong spacing
- modern sans-serif typography
- restrained geometry
- high contrast
- dark-neutral relationship
- information clarity
- visual precision
- minimalism

### allmi

- dark
- black/purple
- productised
- technical
- operational

### Andy Good

- lighter
- more personal
- content-led
- senior-strategy oriented
- white/carbon/yellow

The brands should feel like sibling brands, not the same brand.

## 4. Typography

Use **Instrument Sans throughout**.

### Working hierarchy

| Use | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Major headlines | 650 where variable weights are supported; otherwise 600 |
| Short emphasis / proof numbers | 700 |
| Subheads / navigation / labels | 600 |
| Secondary emphasis | 500 |
| Body copy | 400–500 |

### Typography rules

- sentence case by default
- avoid title case
- minimise spaced all-caps micro-copy
- use tight but controlled headline tracking
- maintain strong hierarchy
- preserve generous horizontal whitespace
- keep vertical rhythm controlled
- avoid oversized header or meta rows
- do not use decorative typography
- do not introduce a second typeface

---

## 5. Graphic language

The graphic language is intentionally minimal and non-decorative.

There is currently no Andy Good logo or monogram. Do not invent one.

Do not use:

- monograms
- invented logos
- decorative connector lines
- criss-cross diagrams
- circles or nodes
- decorative squares
- faux system diagrams
- signal marks as a recurring motif
- geometric decoration for its own sake
- gradients
- generic AI graphics
- SaaS-style UI decoration

Use simple rules, borders or alignment structure only where they improve clarity.

**Typography and composition should carry the identity.**

---

## 6. Photography and imagery

Use:

- real people for trust
- real work for proof
- screenshots only when they support a specific point
- diagrams only when they genuinely explain a system or process
- generated imagery only when it adds conceptual value

### Absolute rule

**Never generate or use an AI-generated portrait or likeness of Andy.**

Do not use:

- robots
- AI brains
- circuitry
- neural networks
- fake UI
- decorative stock imagery
- generic laptop photography
- fake proof

---

## 7. LinkedIn

- keep banners typography-led
- preserve enough safe space for the profile image
- make the main copy dominant
- use Signal Yellow sparingly
- avoid filling empty space with decorative elements
- prioritise mobile readability
- do not include a logo or monogram unless explicitly approved later

### Approved banner copy

**Make complex things clear.**  
**Make clear things convert.**

Supporting line:

**Content strategy · Conversion copy · AI-enabled content systems**

Do not add supporting slogans unless explicitly approved.

---

## 8. andygood.me

- White should dominate
- Carbon should provide visual authority
- Signal Yellow should be used sparingly for action and recognition
- Instrument Sans should carry the full site
- integrate proof and outcomes cleanly
- avoid a SaaS-template aesthetic
- avoid excessive vertical padding
- keep sections compact, sharp and premium

---

## 9. Governing rules

1. Typography first.
2. Whitespace first.
3. Accent second.
4. Real proof over decoration.
5. Repeat principles, not layouts.
6. Do not invent logos or motifs.
7. Do not generate Andy's likeness.
8. Keep Andy visually related to allmi through discipline, not colour matching.

Do not introduce new colours or a second typeface.
