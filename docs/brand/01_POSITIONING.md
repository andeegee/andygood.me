# 01 Positioning

## Primary professional category

**Senior Content & AI Strategist**

Use this as the primary professional category across LinkedIn, andygood.me, proposals, CVs and other public-facing materials unless a specific opportunity requires a narrower title.

## Positioning statement

Andy Good is a **Senior Content & AI Strategist** who helps SaaS, technology and growth-focused businesses improve their messaging, content performance and marketing operations, then build smarter AI-enabled systems behind them.

## Ideal buyers

Primary buyers are senior marketing decision-makers at:

- SaaS companies
- technology companies
- AI companies
- startups and scale-ups
- established businesses modernising content and marketing operations

Typical buyers include founders, CMOs, VPs/Heads of Marketing and Heads of Content.

Agencies are a secondary buyer where they need senior strategic support, embedded content expertise or high-level copy and messaging support.

## Core problems I solve

- Content activity lacks senior strategic coherence.
- Messaging, conversion, SEO, AI search, production and content operations are being handled as separate problems.
- Content and marketing need to perform better commercially.
- Teams need to adapt their content and marketing workflows for AI without sacrificing judgement, quality or brand consistency.
- Businesses need senior content leadership without necessarily hiring a full-time senior employee.

## Differentiation

Andy combines deep commercial experience in content, copywriting, conversion and digital strategy with practical AI systems thinking.

The positioning is not based on being an AI specialist who also understands marketing. It is based on being an experienced marketing, content and conversion strategist who understands how to redesign that work for the AI era.

## Andy vs allmi boundary

### Andy Good

Andy owns work where the primary business problem is related to:

- content
- marketing
- messaging
- conversion
- AI search
- content operations
- marketing knowledge systems
- AI-enabled content and marketing workflows
- growth-related strategy and systems

### allmi

allmi owns broader practical AI implementation across the wider business, including:

- workflow automation
- operational AI
- process redesign
- internal AI systems
- custom applications
- websites and digital systems
- business operations automation
- wider business efficiency

**Boundary rule:** if the primary problem is content, marketing, messaging or growth, it belongs under Andy Good. If the primary problem is broader business operations, it belongs under allmi.

## Positioning guardrails

- Lead with one professional category: **Senior Content & AI Strategist**.
- Preserve discoverability and credibility for senior copywriting, content strategy, messaging, conversion, AI SEO, GEO/AEO, SaaS/technology marketing and content operations.
- Do not abandon commercially valuable copywriting work when the client, rate, strategic value or expansion potential justifies it.
- Do not position Andy as a generic automation consultant, AI engineer, AI developer, no-code automation freelancer or prompt engineer.
- Do not publicly anchor the offer around low-cost Custom GPT builds.
- Do not present long service lists as the primary positioning.
- Avoid commodity production positioning, generic AI claims and low-value pricing anchors.
- Sell systems and commercial outcomes rather than individual AI tools.
- Keep Andy Good and allmi connected but distinct, with separate buyer journeys and commercial purposes.
- AI should strengthen Andy's existing content, marketing and conversion credibility rather than replace it.
